About

Just 30 minutes from Reykjavík, Helgafell is a flat-topped volcanic mountain that formed under a huge ice sheet. From the top you get wide views over Reykjavík and the Reykjanes Peninsula.

Known as the 'holy mountain', Helgafell is full of stories about elves and the hidden people said to live in its rocks. Your guide takes secluded routes away from the tourist trails, where you may spot birds, wildflowers and maybe a shy Arctic fox.

The hike also takes you into nearby lava caves, with a headlamp, for a look at Iceland's volcanic underworld. It's about 8 km with 290 m of climbing, graded moderate, in a group of up to 15.

Frequently asked questions

How hard is the hike?

Moderate: about 8 km with 290 m of elevation gain over 5 hours.

Where do we meet?

At the Helgafell car park on Kaldárselsvegur, 30 minutes from Reykjavík. Pickup can be requested.

What should I bring?

Hiking shoes, waterproof jacket and pants, a packed lunch, 1 L of water, a cap, gloves and a spare t-shirt.

Is there a minimum age?

Yes, the minimum age for this hike is 16.

When does the season start?

Day hikes run from May. The next available dates are in May 2027.
