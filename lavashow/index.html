Full description

Watch real molten lava poured in a purpose-built indoor showroom in Reykjavík’s Grandi district. Hear the cooling rock crackle, see its glow and feel its radiant heat as a live host explains Iceland’s volcanic forces.

CLASSIC EXPERIENCE The 45–55 minute Classic experience includes the full lava show with ground-level seating near the demonstration. Protective glasses are provided. Check the calendar for adult and child ticket choices.

PREMIUM EXPERIENCE The Reykjavík-only Premium experience lasts around 60 minutes and is for guests aged 13 and older. It includes a welcome drink, access to the VIP lounge, second-floor balcony seats, an exclusive behind-the-scenes video and a giftwrapped piece of lava from the show. Elevator access is available.

FAQ

Will I see real molten lava?

Yes. The show demonstrates real molten lava flowing indoors in a controlled showroom.

Is the show affected by the weather?

The experience takes place indoors and runs throughout the year.

Where is the Reykjavík venue?

Lava Show Reykjavík is at Fiskislóð 73, 101 Reykjavík, in the Grandi district.

How do Classic and Premium differ?

Classic is the 45–55 minute lava show with ground-level seating. Premium adds a welcome drink, VIP lounge, balcony seating, a behind-the-scenes video and a lava gift. Premium is for ages 13+ and lasts about 60 minutes.
