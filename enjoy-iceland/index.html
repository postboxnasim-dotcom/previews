Full description

See the northern lights without sharing a bus with strangers. Your guide picks you up from your hotel or apartment in Reykjavík in a private, heated vehicle, and the whole evening is planned around your group.

There's no fixed route. Your guide checks the cloud forecast on the night and drives to the darkest, clearest skies away from the city lights. Stops and viewing time are flexible, so you stay as long as the show is worth it.

While you wait, warm up with hot chocolate made from Icelandic chocolate and milk, with traditional kleinur or kanilbollur. Your guide also helps with your camera settings, so you take home photos of the aurora, not just memories. Enjoy Iceland Tours is family-owned and has run private tours since 2016.

FAQ

What is a private northern lights tour?

Only your group is on board, with your own guide and vehicle. Timing and stops are flexible around you.

Will we see the northern lights?

They're a natural phenomenon, so no tour can guarantee them. Your guide uses the cloud forecast to find your best chance on the night.

When is the best season?

From mid-August to late April. December and January are usually the darkest months.

What time is pickup?

8:30 PM from 1 October to 20 March, and 9:30 PM in September and from 21 March.

How is the price worked out?

It's per group, not per person: ISK 148,900 for 1–4 passengers, up to ISK 295,000 for 14–19.

What should I wear?

Warm layers, a hat, gloves and windproof, waterproof clothing. It gets cold standing outside at night.

Do I need a special camera?

A full-frame camera and a tripod give the best results, and your guide helps with the settings.

Cancellation policy

Weather cancellations

The tour may be cancelled up to 1 hour before departure because of unfavorable weather, and you'll get an update by email. See Enjoy Iceland Tours' refund policy for refund terms.
