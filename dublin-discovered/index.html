Full description

Board at the Sean O'Casey Footbridge, opposite the CHQ Building, and cruise the Liffey between Grattan Bridge in the city centre and the Thomas Clarke Bridge at the edge of Dublin Port.

Along the way you pass under O'Connell Bridge and see Dublin's old Georgian quaysides, including the 18th-century Custom House. The boat then moves through the old port and into the Docklands, where modern architecture sits alongside Georgian warehouses and memorials to Ireland's past. You pass close to the Jeanie Johnston and continue beyond the Samuel Beckett Bridge, where the river opens out, before returning to the start.

Your guide's live commentary makes it fun for all ages, as it has for over fifteen years. The boat seats up to 48, with large windows and a glass roof for good views, and it's wheelchair and buggy accessible.

FAQ

Do I need to book in advance?

Booking online in advance is recommended to guarantee your seats.

How early should we arrive?

Please arrive 10 minutes before departure to check in.

Do I need rain gear?

No. The boat is fully enclosed, with a glass roof and large windows.

Is the tour suitable for children?

Yes, it's suitable and great fun for all ages. Infants under 4 go free.

Can we bring a buggy or wheelchair?

Yes. The boat is fully accessible and your buggy is stored on board during the cruise.

Can we book a private cruise?

Yes. The Spirit of the Docklands is available for private hire. Call +353 1 473 0000 to plan it.
