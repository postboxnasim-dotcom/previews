About

The first stop is Þingvellir National Park, where Iceland's parliament was founded over 1,000 years ago and where you can see the North American and Eurasian plates slowly pulling apart.

Next comes the Geysir geothermal area, where Strokkur erupts almost every five minutes, and then Gullfoss, the 'queen' of Icelandic waterfalls, plunging into a deep gorge. After a lunch break at Gullfoss Café, the Superjeep heads off-road into the highlands and the glacier area of Langjökull, Iceland's second-largest ice cap.

On the way back you stop at Faxi, a small but beautiful waterfall. Snowmobiling on Langjökull can be added for an extra cost, weather permitting. The route may be reversed to avoid the crowds.

Additional info

Know before you book

  • The tour runs all year.
  • Minimum 2 guests on Tue, Thu and Sat, and 3 guests on Mon, Wed, Fri and Sun.
  • Snowmobiling is an optional extra: passengers from age 6, drivers 18+ with a valid licence.
  • Lunch at Gullfoss Café isn't included.
  • Dress warmly: the glacier area is cold and windy.

Frequently asked questions

Is hotel pickup included?

Yes, free pickup from hotels in the Reykjavík capital area, starting at 8:30 AM.

Do we drive on the glacier?

The Superjeep goes off-road into the highlands and glacier area of Langjökull.

Can we go snowmobiling?

Yes, as an optional extra. Passengers from age 6; drivers must be 18+ with a valid licence.

Is lunch included?

No. There's a lunch break at Gullfoss Café, where you can buy food.

Does the tour run in winter?

Yes, all year round, with a minimum of 2–3 guests depending on the day.
