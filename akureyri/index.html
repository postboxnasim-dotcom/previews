Full description

Board right in downtown Akureyri, by the Oddeyrarbót pier next to the round HOF building, and sail into Eyjafjörður, the longest fjord in Iceland. Your trained whale watching guide shares stories about the wildlife, nature, history and environmental challenges of the fjord, and takes photos you can receive after the tour.

The tours run on three large boats built for safe whale watching, including Iceland's only high-speed whale watching ship, with a cinema-style viewing platform at the front. There's plenty of indoor and outdoor seating, toilets on board and a café.

Warm thermal overalls are provided. And if no whales or dolphins appear, you get a free ticket to try again in Akureyri or Reykjavík.

FAQ

Where do we meet?

At the ticket office by Oddeyrarbót pier, next to the round HOF building in downtown Akureyri.

What if we don't see whales?

You get a free ticket to try again on the Classic tour in Akureyri or Reykjavík within 2 years.

Is it warm on board?

There's indoor seating, and warm thermal overalls are provided for time on deck.

Are there toilets on the boat?

Yes, and there's an onboard café selling refreshments.

How long is the tour?

About 3 hours, sometimes 2.5–3.5 depending on where the whales are.

Cancellation policy

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Cancel at least 24 hours before departure for a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours aren't refunded.
