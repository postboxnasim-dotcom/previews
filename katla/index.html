Overview

About this tour

See Iceland's fire and ice in one short adventure. From Vík, a Super Jeep leaves the Ring Road behind and crosses black sand plains between green mountains, a landscape shaped by centuries of eruptions.

At the edge of Kötlujökull, an outlet of the Mýrdalsjökull ice cap, you'll see high walls of ice layered with black ash from the Katla volcano below. After a safety briefing, you put on crampons and a helmet and follow your guide across the glacier into the ice cave, a dome carved by meltwater.

Groups are limited to 14 guests per guide, so there's time for questions about the area's geology and folklore. The meeting point is right off the Ring Road in Vík, about 2.5 hours from Reykjavík.

  • Super Jeep ride across black sand plains
  • Walk into the Katla Ice Cave under Mýrdalsjökull
  • Blue ice layered with black volcanic ash
  • Small groups: up to 14 guests per guide
  • Crampons and helmet included

Frequently asked questions

Where do we meet?

In Vík, in the bus parking lot on the south side of the Icewear/Krónan building (Austurvegur 20), facing the sea.

How long is the tour?

About 3 hours, including the Super Jeep ride, the glacier walk and the ice cave.

Is transport from Reykjavík included?

No. Guests drive to Vík, about 2.5 hours from Reykjavík.

What equipment is provided?

Glacier crampons and a helmet. Bring warm, waterproof clothes and hiking boots.

How fit do I need to be?

The tour suits most travellers with a fair fitness level.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Tours may be changed or cancelled if conditions are unsafe, and you'll get an email.
