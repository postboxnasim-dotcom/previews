About

For those who want to make the most of their day, the FastTrack Katla Ice Cave Tour is the quickest way to experience an authentic ice cave in Iceland, without giving up your whole day.

You meet your local guide at Katlatrack's base in Vík and set off in a powerful Super Jeep across black sand deserts, glacial rivers and landscapes shaped by volcanoes and ice. After about 45–60 minutes you reach the glacier, gear up with helmets, crampons and headlamps, and take a short walk to the cave.

Inside, blue ice is streaked with layers of volcanic ash. Your guide explains how the cave formed and how Iceland's volcanoes shape the glaciers. Groups are 2–12 per guide, and children from 6 are welcome.

Additional info

Know before you book

  • Wear warm clothing, rain gear and hiking boots with ankle support.
  • Difficulty is easy to moderate.
  • Minimum age is 6.
  • The cave changes over time and may look different from photos.

Frequently asked questions

Where do we meet?

At the Katlatrack base, Austurvegur 16, in Vík.

How long is the tour?

About 2.5–3 hours, including 45–60 minutes in the ice cave area.

Can children join?

Yes, from age 6. Children 6–15 have their own price.

What gear is provided?

Helmet, headlamp and crampons. Bring warm, waterproof clothes and hiking boots.

Does it run in winter?

Yes, the FastTrack ice cave tour runs year-round.

Cancellation policy

Cancellation policy

Cancel at least 48 hours before the tour for a refund minus a 5% fee. Cancellations within 48 hours aren't refunded.
