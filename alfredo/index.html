Full description

When the sun goes down, Rome changes. The crowds thin out, the air cools and the monuments light up. This 3-hour golf cart tour shows you the city at its most beautiful, with a local driver-guide at the wheel and no long walks between sights.

Start in the Monti district, glide past the Imperial Fora and stop for photos of the Colosseum lit up at night. Toss a coin into the Trevi Fountain, see the Baroque frescoes of Sant'Ignazio, roll through Piazza Navona and visit the Pantheon, where Raphael is buried. Then head up the Aventine Hill to peek through the famous keyhole at St. Peter's Basilica, and drive past the Altar of the Fatherland on Piazza Venezia.

Along the way, your guide shares the stories, legends and hidden corners most visitors miss. Each cart seats up to 7, so it works for couples, families and small groups, and the route can be adjusted to what you want to see.

FAQ

Do I have to drive the golf cart?

No. A professional local driver-guide drives the whole time, so you can sit back and enjoy the views.

Where do we meet?

Just outside the restricted traffic zone gate at Via Urbana 167, near the Colosseum. Private Tour guests can choose hotel pickup instead.

How long is the tour?

About 3 hours. You can ask to extend it when you book.

What time does the tour start?

Departures are at 5:30 PM and 9:00 PM. If you prefer another time, ask when booking and we'll try to accommodate you.

How many people fit in one golf cart?

Up to 7 per cart. Larger groups travel together in several carts.

Can I join as a solo traveler?

Yes. Solo travelers can join a group tour, or book a private tour just for themselves.

Is the tour suitable for children?

Yes, it's family-friendly and suitable for all ages. Italian law requires child or booster seats for children under 12, and we provide them free.

What safety measures are in place?

Our carts are inspected regularly, every passenger has a seat belt, and our drivers are trained to follow local traffic rules.

What happens if it rains?

Rain covers keep you dry on board. If you'd rather go another day, contact us about rescheduling.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, well-behaved, leashed pets are welcome. Please tell us in advance.

Is the tour wheelchair accessible?

Yes, the tour is wheelchair supported. Message us before booking so we can plan the details with you.

What's the difference between Private and Semi-Private?

Private reserves the whole golf cart for your group and includes hotel pickup. Semi-Private is a more affordable option and starts at Via Urbana 167.
