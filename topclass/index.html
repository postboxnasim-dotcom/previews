Full description

Forget crowded buses and fixed schedules. This private day tour is built around your group, with a dedicated driver-guide and a comfortable luxury vehicle for up to seven people.

The day starts in Belfast at the Peace Walls, where murals and messages of reconciliation tell the story of the city and the Troubles. You then head north to the Dark Hedges, the beech-tree avenue used as the Kingsroad in Game of Thrones, and on to the UNESCO-listed Giant's Causeway, with its hexagonal basalt columns and the legends behind them.

Next come the dramatic cliff-top ruins of Dunluce Castle, an optional stop at the historic Bushmills Distillery, and a scenic drive back along the north coast. Hotel pickup and drop-off are included.

FAQ

Is this a private tour?

Yes. You get your own luxury 7-seat vehicle and a dedicated driver-guide.

Is hotel pickup included?

Yes, pickup and drop-off at your Belfast hotel, or meet at Belfast City Hall.

Is the price per person?

No, it's per vehicle for up to 7 guests.

Can we change the stops?

As it's private, your driver-guide can adapt the day to your group.

Are entrance fees included?

No. Entrance fees, such as the optional Bushmills Distillery, and tips are extra.
